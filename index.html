Mark top-left and bottom-right of the outside card, then top-left and bottom-right of the inside printed frame. Centering uses the distances between these lines. For irregular, borderless, auto-window, and patch designs, use only genuine equivalent frame edges. Results are photo-based estimates and perspective distortion can affect accuracy.
